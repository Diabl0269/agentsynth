## What
Placing an AI song's notes refreshes the timeline once for the whole batch, not once per note.

## Why
Each note placed from an AI song triggered its own timeline refresh. On a 40-track song the apply took 39.5 s; with the refresh coalesced to one per batch it takes about 2 s.

## Tests
- TimelineDocNotesTests covers the single-refresh batch behaviour.
- Local `--fast` gate run from the worktree (see its log).
